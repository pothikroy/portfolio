About / Pothik Roy

Curiosity is
the starting point.

Originally from Nilphamari.
Now based in Dhaka.

I’m Pothik, a video editor and filmmaker working across film, documentary, and television.

I graduated from the University of Dhaka’s Department of Television, Film & Photography in 2026 and am currently pursuing an MSS in the same department.

Editing is the centre of my practice. I enjoy the decisions that give footage its meaning: what to keep, where to cut, when to pause. Alongside editing, I work with scripts and cameras, following a story through different stages of production.

My experience includes D.U. TV packages, freelance projects for government and non-government assignments, and academic films and documentaries. I also work with publication design, reporting, and critical writing about cinema.
