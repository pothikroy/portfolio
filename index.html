02 / The person behind the work

An editor’s eye.
A filmmaker’s curiosity.

I’m interested in how a story finds its form: in a cut, a camera movement, or the space between two images.

My practice spans television packages at D.U. TV, freelance editing, and academic films and documentaries. I studied Television, Film & Photography at the University of Dhaka, where I’m now pursuing my master’s.
